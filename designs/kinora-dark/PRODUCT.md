# Kinora: product truth for design work

What any Kinora page may say, and what it must not claim yet. Design surfaces in this folder read this file; `brief.md` holds the surface briefs and direction contracts.

Source: `docs/V06/ANCS_v0.6_Product_Architecture.md`, `docs/brand/README.md`, `docs/V06/production-readiness-checklist.md`, the V6 milestone records.

- Name and tagline: **Kinora**, "The editor that listens." (proposed brand, D-02; the owner still confirms it).
- You start from footage, an article or a link. The AI editor cuts a first version.
- You change it by speaking (English or Chinese) and by pointing: a moment on the timeline or an area of the picture. "Here" means what you point at.
- Every change arrives as a trial. You keep it or undo it. Originals are never overwritten; the edit history is a log you can step back through.
- Long footage is cut as chapters in parallel; one failed chapter can be redone on its own (V6-10).
- Articles become narrated videos with captions and diagram cards; numbers on screen must come from the article (V6-5).
- Effects are readable code templates the AI picks and tunes, rendered in a sandbox (V6-3).
- Style can be learned from a reference video, optionally (V6-6).
- Review works on a phone: mark moments and areas, leave notes (V6-4).
- Paid generation (generated shots, avatar, voice) shows a quote first, needs your approval, runs once and never retries on its own (V6-7/8).
- A monthly AI spending cap per workspace stops model calls when reached.
- Exports that contain AI generated material carry a visible label and a label in the file metadata (GB 45438; English or Chinese).
- Launch is **invite only** (D-04).

Not settled, so the pages must not claim it: prices (plans exist, all prices empty), legal pages (privacy, terms: blocked on the owner), customers, testimonials, usage numbers, model names, data residency.
