  1. Point instead of hunting

    Say “here” while you mark a moment or drag a box on the picture. Kinora knows the frame and the area you mean.

  2. Every change is a trial

    Each change plays as a preview first, and you keep it or undo it. Your footage is never overwritten, and every step stays in the history.

  3. Long footage, cut in chapters

    Hand over an hour of footage and Kinora plans it as chapters, then cuts them side by side. If one chapter comes out wrong, redo just that one.

  4. Articles become narrated videos

    Paste an article and get narration, captions and diagram cards. A number only goes on screen if it appears in your article.

  5. You decide what gets spent

    Generated shots come with a quote and run only after you approve. They never retry on their own, and a monthly cap stops AI spending.

Questions

If yours is not here, ask it when you request an invite.

What do I need to start?

Footage from your phone or camera, an article, or a link to one. You can also start with a sentence and add footage later.

Which languages can I speak?

English and Chinese, by voice or by typing.

Will Kinora change my original footage?

No. Your files stay as they are. Every change is a trial on the edit, and the history lets you step back to any earlier version.

Will viewers know AI was involved?

When a video contains generated material, such as a narration voice, a generated shot or an effect, the export carries a visible label and a label in the file metadata.

Can it handle long footage?

Yes. Long footage is split into chapters that are cut at the same time, and you can redo one chapter without touching the others.

Can it learn my style?

If you want it to. Show it a reference video and Kinora measures its pacing and cuts into a style it can follow. Without one, it judges from your footage.

Can I review a cut on my phone?

Yes. Open the review page on your phone, mark a moment or an area and leave a note. Kinora turns each note into a trial.

How much does it cost?

Plans are not priced yet. During the beta a monthly cap stops AI spending, and anything paid, like a generated shot, shows a quote before it runs.

Who can join the beta?

The beta is invite only. Leave your email and we send an invite when a seat opens.
